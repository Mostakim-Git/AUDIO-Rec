AUDIO-rec :: branding
=====================

audiorec-logo-user.svg        the author's logo, exactly as supplied - SOURCE OF TRUTH
audiorec-logo-user-flat.svg   same artwork, CSS turned into attributes (tools/svg_flatten.py)
audiorec-lockup-user.svg      logo + "AUDIO-rec" wordmark, for docs and store listings
audiorec-mark-user.png        transparent raster of the logo (README, docs)
audiorec-brand-preview.png    the icon under square / circle / squircle masks

Generated from the above by tools/gen_brand.py:
  app/src/main/res/mipmap-*/ic_launcher_foreground.png   adaptive-icon foreground (108dp)
  app/src/main/res/mipmap-anydpi-v26|v33/ic_launcher*.xml adaptive-icon manifests
  app/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/brand_logo.png          full-colour logo for the UI

The launcher plate is warm paper (#FFF7F5F1, values/colors.xml) because the
artwork is black line art with warm ink: on the app's dark panel the outline and
the waveform disappear.

Legacy one-colour marks (still generated by tools/gen_logo.py, not the brand):
  audiorec-logo.svg, audiorec-mark.svg, audiorec-lockup.svg,
  res/drawable/ic_logo.xml (tinted in the sidebar/dashboard)
  res/drawable/ic_launcher_mono.xml (themed-icon layer)
